=== CORE PRODUCTS ===

1. UC CLIENT (Unified Communications Client)
A single-interface platform integrating voice, video, messaging, and collaboration. Includes:
- Standard SMS: One-to-one texting from your VoIP extension. Ideal for solo users, field reps, and individual agents. Super simple setup — enable on your extension and start texting instantly.
- Mobile Application: Full business phone system on iOS/Android. Make/receive calls, text, join video meetings, visual voicemail, contact directory. Works over Wi-Fi or cellular data.
- Video Conferencing & Contact Sharing: HD video meetings for up to 100 participants with no time limits. Screen sharing, virtual rooms, ad-hoc and scheduled meetings. No separate Zoom/Teams needed.

2. CONTACT CENTER
Multi-channel customer engagement via voice, email, live chat, and text. Features IVR, call routing, reporting, analytics, and API integrations. Includes Workforce Management tools for staff scheduling and optimization.

3. PBX-SIP TRUNKING
Connects existing on-premise PBX systems to the internet via SIP protocol. Unlimited SIP trunk groups, unlimited pre-paid call paths, ability to burst up to 10 additional call paths on demand. Saves up to 40% over traditional telephone services.

4. E-FAX (Electronic Faxing)
Digital faxing via email or web portal. Eliminates fax machines, reduces paper, enhances security. Send and receive faxes from any device.

5. ENTERPRISE CLOUD SMS (Cloud Messaging)
Two tiers:
- Starter: Shared team inboxes, contacts management, canned responses — perfect for teams starting with shared texting.
- Professional: Everything in Starter plus Blasts (bulk messaging), Drip Campaigns (automated text sequences), and Reminders (appointment confirmations, follow-ups). 98%+ text open rates.

6. API INTEGRATION
Integrate VoIP with CRM, ERP, helpdesk, and business applications. Automates workflows, improves data synchronization. Available on the Elite plan.
