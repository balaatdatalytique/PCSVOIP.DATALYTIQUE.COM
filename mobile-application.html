Mobile Application

MOBILE APP

Take Your Business Phone System Anywhere

Your all-in-one VoIP companion for smartphones – make calls, send texts, join video meetings, and stay connected on the go. Our mobile application turns your smartphone (iOS or Android) into a full-featured extension of your PCS VoIP business phone system. Download it once, log in with your extension credentials, and access everything you need to run your business from anywhere.

Core Capabilities

  • Make and receive business calls — Use your company phone number for inbound/outbound calls over Wi-Fi or cellular data — crystal-clear VoIP quality, no extra carrier charges.
  • Send and receive messages — Text (SMS/MMS) directly from your business number — share photos, documents, or quick updates without switching apps.
  • Join video meetings — Host or participate in HD video calls and conferences right in the app — perfect for client check-ins, team huddles, or remote demos.
  • Visual voicemail — Access, play, and manage voicemails on the go — listen, read transcriptions, delete, or forward messages easily.
  • Contact directory & history — Sync your business contacts, view call logs, recent activity, and company directories.
  • Additional productivity tools — Call transfer, hold, mute, speaker/Bluetooth support, presence status, and more.

Key Benefits

  • True mobility for your team — Work from anywhere without missing calls or messages — field reps, remote workers, and hybrid teams stay fully connected.
  • Seamless business continuity — Calls and texts route to the app even if you're away from your desk phone.
  • Professional image on the move — Always present your business number — customers see your company line, not a personal cell.
  • Cost savings & simplicity — No need for separate mobile plans or devices; leverage your existing VoIP subscription.
  • Easy setup & secure access — Quick download from App Store/Google Play, secure login, and works over any strong internet connection.
  • Boost productivity — Handle calls, texts, and meetings in one app — reduce app-switching and keep everything business-focused.
