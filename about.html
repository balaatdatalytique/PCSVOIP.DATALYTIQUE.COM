About Us

Who We Are

Empowering Businesses with Reliable Solutions.

Based at 12195 S Strang Line Rd, Kansas, Pegasus Communication Solutions (PCS) provides telecommunication and conferencing solutions to businesses of all sizes nationwide, serving industries like healthcare, manufacturing, and education.

Specializing in business phone services, PCS VoIP offers cost-effective, flexible communication solutions for industries such as healthcare, retail, and finance.

We focus on enhancing productivity and exceeding customer expectations with tailored features and custom solutions.

Our Vision

Redefining Business Communication Excellence

PCS VoIP is dedicated to delivering the highest quality of customizable Unified Communication technology, nationally concentrated on a customer centric organization and culture.

As a nationwide telecommunications provider, PCS VoIP understands that businesses face increasing budgetary constraints. Our passion is helping our customers to reduce operating costs while reaching higher standards of productivity through our products and services. Let us be an extension of your core team and handle your business VoIP so that you can spend your time and talents elsewhere. As business VoIP providers, we work tirelessly to ensure your communications are running efficiently 24/7.
