Pegasi AI ChatBot

AI-POWERED CHATBOT

Your 24/7 Website Sales Assistant

Pegasi AI ChatBot engages every website visitor with intelligent, context-aware conversations. Powered by advanced AI with deep knowledge of your business, Pegasi answers questions, qualifies leads, and converts visitors — around the clock.

Core Features

Context-Aware Conversations

Trained on your complete business knowledge base — services, pricing, industries, and policies. Every answer is accurate and on-brand.

Instant Engagement

Greets visitors within seconds of arrival with a friendly popup. No waiting, no searching — immediate help when they need it.

Lead Capture

Naturally collects visitor contact information during conversations. Qualifies interest level and routes hot leads to your sales team.

Multi-Session Memory

Maintains conversation history within each session. Visitors get contextual, coherent follow-up answers — not repetitive responses.

Seamless Handoff

When visitors need human help, Pegasi connects them to your team via phone (844-PCS-VOIP) or transfers to live support.

Always Learning

Continuously updated with your latest services, pricing, and features. Your chatbot stays current as your business evolves.

How Pegasi ChatBot Works

1

Visitor Arrives

Pegasi greeting popup appears within seconds, welcoming the visitor and offering immediate assistance.

2

Conversation Begins

Visitor types or clicks to start chatting. Pegasi engages naturally with friendly, professional responses.

3

Intelligent Response

AI responds with accurate, contextual answers from your knowledge base — delivering the right information every time.

Key Benefits

Handles Unlimited Concurrent Conversations

No matter how many visitors are on your site, Pegasi engages every single one simultaneously without delays.

Available on Every Page of Your Website

Pegasi follows visitors across your entire site, ready to help no matter where they navigate.

No Coding Required

Knowledge base managed server-side. Update your chatbot's knowledge without touching a single line of code.

Professional, Consistent Brand Voice

Every response reflects your brand's tone and messaging, ensuring a polished experience for every visitor.
