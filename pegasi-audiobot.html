Pegasi AI AudioBot

VOICE-POWERED AI ASSISTANT

Speak Naturally. Get Instant Answers.

Pegasi AI AudioBot brings voice-first AI interaction to your website. Visitors click a microphone icon and speak naturally — Pegasi listens, understands, and responds with a natural AI voice in real-time. No phone calls needed, no typing required.

CORE FEATURES

What Makes AudioBot Powerful

Real-Time Voice Streaming

Advanced speech-to-speech AI processes voice in real-time via WebSocket streaming. Sub-second latency for natural, fluid conversations.

Natural AI Voice

Multiple natural-sounding AI voices (Ara, Eve, Leo, Rex, Sal). Choose the voice that best represents your brand.

Browser-Native

Works directly in the visitor's web browser using the device microphone. No downloads, no plugins, no phone calls required.

Business Knowledge

Loaded with your complete business context — services, pricing, industries, FAQs. Every voice answer is accurate and relevant.

Live Transcription

Real-time transcript displayed as the conversation happens. Visitors can read along while listening to Pegasi's voice.

Secure Architecture

Voice streams through a secure server-side proxy. API keys never exposed to the browser. Enterprise-grade WebSocket security.

HOW IT WORKS

Four Simple Steps

1

Click the Microphone

Visitor clicks the mic icon on the Pegasi chat popup or in the chat window.

2

Grant Mic Access

Browser requests microphone permission. One-click approval to start.

3

Speak Naturally

Talk to Pegasi like a human assistant. Ask about services, pricing, or anything about the business.

4

Hear the Answer

Pegasi responds with natural AI voice in real-time. See the transcript scroll as it speaks.

SPECIFICATIONS

Technical Specifications

Audio Format PCM16 at 24kHz
Latency <1 second response
AI Model Grok 4.1 Fast (Realtime)
Protocol WebSocket (WSS)
Voice Activity Detection Server-side VAD with configurable sensitivity
Session Limit 30 minutes per session
Concurrent Sessions Unlimited for website visitors
Browser Support Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari (requires HTTPS)
USE CASES

Who Benefits from AudioBot

Accessibility

Voice interaction for visitors who prefer speaking over typing, or those with accessibility needs.

Quick Answers

Faster than typing — visitors get instant voice responses to complex questions about your services.

Mobile Visitors

Ideal for mobile users who find typing on small screens difficult. Just tap and talk.
